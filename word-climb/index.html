How to play

Climb from the start word to the goal word by changing one letter at a time. Every rung must be a real word.

Tap a tile, then tap a letter to change it. Hit ENTER to climb. green = letter in the right spot of the goal, yellow = in the goal, wrong spot.

Each puzzle has a par. Finish in fewer moves to beat it, then share your climb — no spoilers, just colors.

Feeling strong? Switch between 4-letter and 5-letter daily climbs with the toggle up top — separate puzzles, separate streaks.

Word ladders were invented by Lewis Carroll in 1877. He'd have crushed your streak.
